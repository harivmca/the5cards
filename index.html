HOW TO PLAY

  • Each player gets 5 cards. Goal: keep your total points as low as possible.
  • On your turn: drop a card (or same-rank pair), then pick from the Open Card or the Deck.
  • Your dropped card appears in the staging area — it becomes the Open Card once you pick. You cannot take it back.
  • Same-rank pair (e.g. 9♥ & 9♠): click either to auto-select both, drop both at once!
  • Joker (2 in the deck, shown as 🃏) = 0 points — the most valuable card to hold!
  • Ace = 1 point (low value — great to keep!). 10, J, Q, K = 10 pts each.
  • Hit SHOW to freeze — lowest hand wins the round; others add their pts to their score.
  • Reach 500 points → eliminated. Deck empty → discard pile reshuffled automatically.
